> *Example:* The story opens with arithmetic rather than with a character: "One dollar and eighty-seven cents" is repeated, and Della "counted it" three times. ## 2. Initial impression

[One paragraph. What do you think the story is *about*, based only on these lines?]

> *Example:* My impression is that the story stages a contest between two economies. One is a market economy in which love must be counted, priced and paid for. The other is a gift economy in which value is measured by what one is willing to lose. The movement from the first to the second seems to pass through the commodification of the body (hair) and of inheritance (the watch).
